# @indexfast: what the account already posts

Read through the Instagram API on 2026-11-13: the 72 most recent posts, dated 8–13 November 2026. Likes and comments only; reach and watch time were not pulled.

## Volume

- 67 reels and 5 carousels in six days: 11 to 13 posts every day.
- Another automated pipeline is already publishing here. Anything this project adds is on top of that.

## Content types

| Type | Share | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| SEO myth or mistake | Most posts | "Your [tool / agency / file] says X. Google never did that." One concept per reel: canonical tags, crawl budget, robots.txt, soft 404, FAQ schema, Domain Authority |
| How search works | Many | "Google never searches the internet." Short explainers of indexing stages, passage ranking, Discover |
| Search and AI-search news | Some | Core update dates, changed Search Console help text, ChatGPT business profiles |
| General AI and design news | About 8 | Claude, Figma, Adobe, quantum results. Not about search |
| Product | Rare | The MCP server reel is the clearest one |

## What this suggests

These are readings of a small sample, not proven causes.

- Volume is not buying reach. Twelve posts a day at one like each is a weak signal to send the platform; fewer, better reels are worth testing.
- The best post is the one closest to what IndexFast does (submitting sitemaps). Topics on indexing and getting pages found should be favoured over general SEO trivia.
- General AI news does not fit the account and scored lowest.
- Almost no post shows the product. A reel that shows the fix, not only the problem, is the gap.

## Direction for new reels

1. Stay on indexing: discovery, sitemaps, IndexNow, "why is my page not found".
2. Each reel ends on what IndexFast does about it, in one line, with the free plan as the call to action.
3. Keep every claim inside `brand.md`.
