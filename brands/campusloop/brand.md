# CampusLoop

- Site: https://campusloop.space/
- Instagram: @campusloop.space (Composio account alias `campusloop.space`)
- Tagline: "Your campus. Only your campus."
- Audience: students at Indian colleges.
- Voice: playful, casual, student-to-student. Hinglish is fine in small doses.
- CTA: "Join with college email" → campusloop.space (link in bio)
- Visual style: not yet recorded. On the first run, read the site's CSS, record the colors and fonts here, and save a logo and 3–5 screenshots to `assets/`.

## Facts that may be used (source: the site, 2026-11-13)

- Sign-up with a college email limits each student to their own campus. A personal email gets a read-only Campus Preview.
- Anonymous confessions: the author's name never shows, not even to staff. Identity recovery needs a lawful order or a serious safety report, and is logged.
- Campus polls.
- Events with reminders: fests, talks, club meetups.
- Notes, past papers and free courses by branch, all free to open. Courses include CS50. Finishing a course gives a CampusLoop certificate.
- Campus Match: mutual-like feature for verified students aged 18+.
- Loop Points for inviting friends.
- Reporting: three reports hide a post until a person reviews it within 24 hours.
- 1,441 colleges listed. 9,269 notes and past papers. Re-check both numbers on the site before use.
- Free to join. Built to follow India's DPDP Act, 2023.

## Never do

- Never show a real confession, a real student, or a real college's name as if the post came from there. Every confession, poll and result in a reel is written for the reel and is fictional.
- Never invent user counts, poll results presented as real, or testimonials.
- Campus Match reels stay clean and state 18+.
