# @campusloop.space: what the account already posts

Read through the Instagram API on 2026-11-13: all 40 posts returned, dated 28 August to 12 November 2026. The account had 207 followers. Likes and comments only; reach and watch time were not pulled.

| When | What | Likes |
|---|---|---|

| 10–12 November | 21 narrated story reels: "In my college…", hostel horror, riddles, anonymous-confession stories | Median 9, range 1–45 |

## What this suggests

These are readings of a small sample, not proven causes.

- An impossible rule tied to a real college pain (attendance, ID cards, mess food, placements) beats a generic town story.
- Riddles and "pause and comment" puzzles scored lowest.
- Stories that end on an anonymous confession or a lost-and-found post show the product inside the plot, and held up well (14–21 likes).
- The single best post was a real one about building the product at a real campus. Real founder and campus content is under-used.

## Direction for new reels

1. Keep the "In this college, [rule]" story format, built with `engine/storyreel`.
2. Pick rules from everyday Indian college life: attendance, hostel curfew, mess, backlogs, placements, group projects.
3. Let the plot turn on something CampusLoop does: an anonymous confession, a poll, a lost-and-found post, shared notes.
4. All confessions, names and colleges in a story are fictional, and the caption says so.
